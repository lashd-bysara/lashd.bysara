A LITTLE PREP GOES A LONG WAY

Before Your
Appointment

Arriving prepared helps us make the most of your appointment and achieve the best possible result.

01

Clean Face

Please arrive with a clean face and no makeup or skincare around the eyes.

02

No Mascara

Please avoid mascara on the day of your appointment.

03

No Lash Curlers

Avoid using lash curlers before your appointment.

04

No Oily Products

Avoid oily skincare and products around the eye area.

05

Contact Lenses

Please remove contact lenses before your appointment.

06

No Strip Lash Glue

Please arrive without strip lashes or residual strip lash glue.

07

Be On Time

Arrive on time so we can enjoy your full appointment together.

KEEP YOUR LASHES HAPPY

Lash
Aftercare

A little care goes a long way in keeping your lashes looking beautiful.

01

Keep Them Clean

Gently cleanse your lashes daily with a lash shampoo or gentle cleanser to remove oil, dirt and makeup build-up.

02

Avoid Oil-Based Products

Avoid oily skincare, makeup removers and mascara around the eyes as these can weaken the adhesive bond.

03

Brush Daily

Use a clean spoolie to gently brush your lashes daily and keep them neat and in place.

04

Sleep Smart

Try to sleep on your back or side and avoid sleeping directly on your face to help prevent lash damage.

05

Avoid Water & Steam

Avoid water, steam, saunas and heavy sweating for the first 24 hours after your appointment.

06

Book Regular Infills

Infills every 2–3 weeks help maintain a full, fresh and flawless set.

07

Be Gentle

Avoid rubbing, picking or pulling your lashes. Be gentle when cleansing around the eyes.

WHY INFILLS MATTER

Don't leave your extensions longer than 3 weeks.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked
Questions

01 How long do lash extensions last? +

With proper care, lash extensions last around 2–3 weeks. Regular infills will keep them looking full and fresh.

02 Will extensions damage my natural lashes? +

No — when applied correctly and cared for properly, extensions should not damage your natural lashes.

03 Can I shower with lash extensions? +

Yes. Please avoid water, steam and heavy sweating for the first 24 hours following your appointment.

04 Can I wear mascara with lash extensions? +

Mascara is not recommended as it can weaken the adhesive bond and cause product build-up.

05 How often should I get infills? +

Every 2–3 weeks is ideal to maintain a full and fresh set.

06 Can I swim with lash extensions? +

Yes, but please wait 24 hours after your appointment before swimming.

07 Can I cry with lash extensions? +

Yes. Lash extensions are waterproof once the initial 24-hour aftercare period has passed.

08 Can I wear eyeliner? +

Yes. Avoid the waterline and oil-based products around your lash extensions.
